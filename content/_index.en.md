## Areas of Expertise

### RAG and generative AI

I work on Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems that connect large language models with reliable knowledge sources, with a focus on retrieval quality.

### Information retrieval

I build solutions around search engines, document ranking, semantic search, and helping people find the right information quickly.

### Digital archives

My interests include digitization, metadata management, OCR workflows, and institutional archive applications.

### Software development

I create sustainable, searchable, and practical software solutions for library and archive use cases.
